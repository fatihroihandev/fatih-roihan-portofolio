---

## Motion Principles

Purpose:
Guide user attention, not impress the user.

Rules:

- Animation under 300ms
- Subtle Fade & Slide
- No Auto Carousel
- No Flashy Effects
- Motion should improve usability

# Design Philosophy

Every design decision must support trust.

The goal of the website is not to impress visitors with visuals, but to build credibility, communicate value, and encourage meaningful business conversations.
