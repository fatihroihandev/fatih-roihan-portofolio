Process

From first message to launched website.

A simple process so you always know what happens next. Timeline and number of revisions are agreed per project before work starts.

  1. Discuss your needs

    Tell me about your business or brand, your goal, and who the website is for.

  2. Define scope and estimate

    We agree on pages, features, and content, then I share an estimate for your project.

  3. Design and build

    I build the website and share progress so you can give feedback along the way.

  4. Review and revise

    You review the result and I make revisions within the scope we agreed.

  5. Launch and hand over

    The website goes live and I hand over the files and explain how it works.

FAQ

Questions before starting a project?

Still have a question? Ask on WhatsApp

I'm not sure what kind of website I need. Can you help?

Yes. You do not need a technical specification. Tell me about your business or brand and what you want visitors to do, and we can decide together whether a landing page, company profile, or portfolio site fits best.

What kinds of websites do you build?

Landing pages, business and company profile websites, and portfolio or personal brand websites. I can also improve an existing website, and I build custom web systems and internal tools.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the number of pages, features, and how ready your content is. Once the scope is clear, I give you a realistic timeline before we start.

How much does a website cost?

I do not list fixed prices because projects differ in pages, features, and content. Share your needs on WhatsApp and I will send an estimate based on the scope.

Can you improve or redesign my existing website?

Yes, for display issues, responsiveness flaws, broken interactions, and performance bottlenecks. I conduct an initial technical assessment first, then clarify the scope of fixes and recommend optimal enhancements.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Projects are discussed and managed online, mainly through WhatsApp, with progress shared along the way.
