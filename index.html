FAQ

Questions before starting a project?

Here are some common questions about working together and building digital solutions for your business.

Do I need to know exactly what system I need?

No. You do not need to have a complete technical solution in mind. We can start by discussing your current process, the problems you are facing, and what you want to improve. From there, I can help identify the most practical solution.

What types of projects can you help with?

I help build custom business systems, internal tools, workflow automation, management applications, tracking systems, company websites, and other web-based solutions designed around specific business needs.

How does a project usually start?

We start with a discussion about your business, current workflow, challenges, and goals. After understanding the problem, I help define the scope, propose a suitable solution, and determine the next steps.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope and complexity of the project. A simple website or focused internal tool may take less time than a larger business system with multiple workflows. The timeline will be discussed after the project scope is understood.

Can you improve an existing system or workflow?

Yes. Not every problem requires building something from scratch. I can help analyze existing workflows, identify inefficiencies, and improve or extend existing systems where appropriate.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Projects can be discussed and managed remotely through online communication and collaboration tools.
